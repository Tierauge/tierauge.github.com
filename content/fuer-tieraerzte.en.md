We see ourselves as an ophthalmic complement to the referring practice – not as a replacement for the
existing primary care. Specialised diagnostics and treatment are carried out in close consultation with
the referring practice. Once treatment is complete, findings, treatment plan and recommendations are
sent back and the patient is referred back to their family vet.

## Process

1. Referral or direct contact
2. Ophthalmic examination
3. Findings and treatment planning
4. Structured report
5. Referral back

- helpful previous findings
