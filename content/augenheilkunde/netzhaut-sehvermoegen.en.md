Sudden or gradual loss of vision has many possible causes – from a primary retinal disease to a general
illness such as high blood pressure that shows up in the eye first.

## When you should see us

- your pet suddenly bumps into objects or can no longer find its way in the dark
- conspicuously wide pupils that don't react to light – an alarm signal, especially in older cats
- a gradual deterioration in vision noticed by you or your family vet
- known kidney disease, thyroid disease or high blood pressure

## What we examine

With ophthalmoscopy we examine the retina for detachment, bleeding, inflammation or degeneration.

causes of sudden blindness in older cats – is behind it. If necessary, we add an ocular ultrasound or a
retinal function test (electroretinogram) to distinguish between a disease of the eye and a cause in
the brain or optic nerves. Further therapy is closely coordinated with your family vet.
