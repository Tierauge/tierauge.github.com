An ophthalmic examination is far more thorough than the quick look into the eye during a routine vet
visit: with a slit lamp, ophthalmoscope and special measuring devices, changes can be detected that are
invisible to the naked eye – often before your pet shows any symptoms.

That is why every treatment starts with a complete examination: a vision test, checking the pupillary
response, the highly magnifying slit lamp, a look at the retina, measurement of intraocular pressure and
tear production, and a staining test of the ocular surface. Where necessary, we add an examination of
the drainage angle, an ocular ultrasound or a retinal function test. How this examination works in
detail is shown step by step with photos in our knowledge base article
[Die Augenuntersuchung](/wissensbank/augenuntersuchung/) (in German).
