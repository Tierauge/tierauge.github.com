The inner eye includes the iris, the anterior chamber and the lens. The most common condition that
owners notice themselves is cataract – a clouding of the lens that can lead to blindness.

## When you should see us

- a greyish or whitish cloudiness in the pupil
- a sudden deterioration in vision, for example bumping into furniture or uncertainty in the dark
- your pet has diabetes – diabetes can lead to cataract very quickly
- a red, painful eye with a small pupil as a possible sign of inflammation inside the eye (uveitis)

## What we examine

We assess the lens, iris and anterior chamber with the slit lamp and check for inflammation inside the
eye or a displaced lens. Before any possible surgery, we also use ultrasound and a retinal function
test (electroretinogram) to check whether the retina behind the clouded lens is still intact – this is
decisive for whether surgery can really benefit vision.

## Cataract surgery

We perform cataract surgery – removing the clouded lens and implanting an artificial lens – at our
practice in Greifswald. Whether and when surgery makes sense for your pet, we discuss with you in detail
after the preliminary examination: the stage of the cataract, general health and the result of the
retinal function test are the most important factors.

More about the different forms and causes of cataract can be found in our [knowledge base](/wissen/)
(in German).
