This website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a service of GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200,
1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands, and GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco,
CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the website, GitHub automatically processes technical data sent by your browser.
This includes in particular your **IP address**, which according to GitHub is logged and stored
for security purposes; the date and time of the request, the page requested, the browser type and
the referring page may also be recorded. This data is technically necessary to deliver the
website and to keep it secure and stable. I have no access to these logs myself and do not

(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the
**EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework**, on the basis of which the European Commission has found an
adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR). More information:
[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The connection to this website is encrypted via **HTTPS (TLS)**.

## 4. Google Maps (only with your consent)

On the homepage and on the "Locations & contact" page you can display maps from **Google Maps**.
The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

The maps are **only loaded once you click "Load map"**. Before that, no data is sent to Google.
After the click, your browser connects to Google's servers, transmitting among other things your
IP address, the page visited and technical browser information to Google. Google may also use
cookies or similar technologies. If you are signed in to a Google account, Google can link the
visit to your account.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Your consent is not
stored and only applies to the current page view; on your next visit the maps again load only
after a click. You can withdraw it for the future at any time by reloading the page.

Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA in the process. Google LLC is certified under the
**EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework** (Art. 45 GDPR). More information:
[Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

Alternatively, you can use the "Open in Google Maps" link to view the locations directly on
Google; in that case only Google's terms apply.

## 5. Settings stored locally

If you switch the colour scheme (light/dark) or choose how the overview in the "Wissen" area is
displayed, this setting is saved in your **browser's local storage** ("localStorage") so that it
is kept for your next visit. This information never leaves your device, is not transmitted to me
or to third parties and contains no personal data. You can delete it at any time in your browser
settings.

The legal basis is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for a service you have
explicitly requested).

## 6. Contact by email or phone

If you contact me by email or phone, I process the information you provide (e.g. name, contact
details, your request and, where applicable, information about your animal) in order to answer
your enquiry and, if necessary, arrange an appointment.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry concerns an examination or treatment, and
otherwise my legitimate interest in answering your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete your
information as soon as it is no longer needed for this purpose, unless statutory retention
obligations apply.

I use the **Gmail** service of Google Ireland Limited (address above) for email. Emails sent to
me are therefore processed on Google's servers; a transfer to Google LLC in the USA cannot be
ruled out (certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). Unencrypted emails can also, in
principle, be read by third parties in transit. **For confidential information, please use the
phone instead.**
